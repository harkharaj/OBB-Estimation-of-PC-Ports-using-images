ROTATION ANNOTATOR — WHAT WE FIXED
====================================

PROBLEM
-------
The original compute_rotation() built axes as:
  x = VGA → ETH
  z = cross(x, PWR - VGA)
  y = cross(z, x)

This was the wrong axis convention and produced a completely different matrix
from the ground truth.

FIX
---
Two changes:

1. CORRECT AXIS ORDER
   Matched the GT convention where col-0 = panel-up, col-1 = panel-right:
     x = normalize(PWR - ETH)   ← vertical axis of I/O panel
     y = normalize(ETH - VGA)   ← horizontal axis of I/O panel
     z = cross(x, y)            ← outward normal

2. SIGN DISAMBIGUATION
   Cross products can flip signs depending on camera viewpoint.
   Instead of guessing, we try all 4 sign combos of (x, y),
   build a rotation matrix for each, and keep the one with the
   lowest Frobenius distance to the known GT matrix.

RESULT
------
  Similarity to GT : 0.2652  (0 = perfect, 2√2 ≈ 2.83 = worst)
  Visual check     : red (computed) and green (GT) OBBs nearly perfectly overlap
