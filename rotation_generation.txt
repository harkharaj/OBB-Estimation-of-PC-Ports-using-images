ROTATION ANNOTATOR — METHODOLOGY
==================================

OVERVIEW
--------
We built a Flask-based annotation tool that lets you click the center of three
known sockets (VGA, Ethernet, Power) across multiple frames. From these 2D pixel
clicks, it triangulates the 3D world-space position of each socket center, then
constructs the panel rotation matrix.

TRIANGULATION
-------------
For each socket, you click its center on at least 2 frames. Each click gives a
2D pixel coordinate (u, v) on a known camera pose. Using the camera intrinsics K
and the pose for each frame, we build a linear system (DLT) and solve it with SVD
to recover the 3D world position of that socket.

ROTATION MATRIX CONSTRUCTION
------------------------------
With three triangulated points — VGA (p_vga), Ethernet (p_eth), Power (p_pwr) —
we define the panel's local coordinate axes as follows:

  x (panel up / vertical axis)   = normalize(p_pwr - p_eth)
  y (panel right / horizontal)   = normalize(p_eth - p_vga)
  z (panel outward normal)       = normalize(x cross y)

We then re-orthogonalise using Gram-Schmidt (y = z cross x) to ensure a clean
right-handed coordinate frame, and try all 4 sign combinations of (x, y) to
select the one that produces a geometrically consistent outward-facing normal.

The final rotation matrix R = [x | y | z] (columns) maps from panel-local space
to world space.
